# Design System — Paste (formerly Badmouth)

## Product Context
- **What this is:** UK subscription-first natural toothpaste in a raw aluminium tube. £16 per 2-month shipment (26p/day), letterbox delivery, £5 refundable founding-member deposit as the first CTA.
- **Who it's for (REPOSITIONED 2026-07-03, founder-set):** health-conscious 25–40s in the UK + Europe who are STILL USING mainstream toothpaste (Colgate/Sensodyne) and haven't yet switched to natural. We convert new customers INTO the category via education — we do not fight Pärla/Happier for their existing customers (they have scale and cheaper COGS; that fight loses). The enemy is the WORSE product (mainstream paste: SLS foam-theatre, plastic tubes), never rival natural brands. Existing natural-buyers arrive later via word of mouth. Copy is British English throughout.
- **Space/industry:** DTC oral care. Competitors verified 2026-07-02: Happier (lavender + Playfair serif, eco-polite), Pärla (baby-blue pastel, Beatrice grotesque), Georganics (kraft/earth), Truthpaste, ZING. Attitude benchmarks: Liquid Death (do NOT imitate), Monte (extended-sans premium minimal).
- **Project type:** Marketing site / landing pages first (deposit page), then content templates, packaging, and the pitch deck re-skin.
- **The memorable thing (north star):** "Toothpaste with a middle finger" — BUT the finger lives in the words and behavior, never in ugly graphics. The design itself is sunny, beautiful, premium.

## Name
- **PASTE** (working styling: **Paste!** in the wordmark — Fraunces 900, tomato red). Chosen 2026-07-03; the founder loved the placeholder.
- **⚠️ Clearance status: NOT yet cleared.** "Paste" is descriptive for toothpaste → weak trademark position by default. First-pass web sweep (2026-07-03) found no UK toothpaste brand named "Paste" (closest: Truthpaste, ZING). TODO before spending on packaging: UK IPO register search (Class 3 + 21), domain strategy (paste.com unattainable — candidates: getpaste.co.uk, usepaste.com, paste.club), and distinctive-styling registration (word + logo mark, the "Paste!" lockup is more registrable than the bare word).
- Old name Badmouth killed 2026-07-03: negative connotation, wrong for ICP, too jokey to grow, vibe shifted.

## Aesthetic Direction
- **Direction:** RETRO-FRESH ON WHITE — 70s Kodak sunshine warmth (chunky soft Fraunces, tomato red, butter tones) sitting on a clean white premium base. Direction B's charm with a gallery-white spine; the "crazy colours" stay inside photography and stage panels, never as page backgrounds.
- **Decoration level:** Intentional — one rotated price badge, warm photo panels with rounded corners, everything else calm.
- **Mood:** A beautiful sunny morning that happens to be telling you the truth. Joyful, collectible, premium — never twee, never corporate, never grim.
- **Rejected directions (archived in ~/.gstack/projects/dabuildasoftweh-Founder/designs/):** Gutter Press (2026-07-02→03, "ugly, wrong for ICP"), Industrial Spec, Beautiful Object (A), Swiss Apothecary (C), full-butter Retro-Fresh (B as-is — backgrounds too loud).
- **Approved reference:** `designs/design-system-20260703/paste-b-on-white.html` (+ .png).

## Voice (HARD RULE — founder-set, survives all redesigns)
**Brands behave values; they don't say them.** The design is sunny; the words are dry; the evidence does the accusing.
- ✅ "Never think about toothpaste again." / "No fake foam. No plastic tube. No claim without a printed source."
- ❌ Self-praise ("the only honest toothpaste"), attack copy, shouting.
- **Receipts discipline:** no claim without a numbered source. Known-dead ammo: "plastic IS in the paste" (UK microbead ban 2018). Live ammo: SLS foam-is-theatre (say "may", cite J. Oral Path. Med. 2019 systematic review), plastic tube landfill, aluminium recyclability, 26p/day maths.

## Typography
Two fonts, two jobs — both free Google Fonts:
- **Display/Hero/Wordmark:** Fraunces (variable; SOFT 100, wght 800–900, italic for emphasis words) — chunky, warm, charming, premium. The wordmark is `Paste!` in Fraunces 900.
- **Body/UI:** Work Sans (400–700) — clean, friendly, invisible.
- **Evidence/citations:** Work Sans 400 italic at caption size with a superscript number — sources stay elegant, not photocopied.
- **Loading:** `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Fraunces:ital,opsz,wght,SOFT@0,9..144,300..900,100;1,9..144,400..900,100&family=Work+Sans:wght@400..700&display=swap`
- **Scale:** hero 52–64px (Fraunces) · section 28–34px (Fraunces) · body 16–18px (Work Sans) · captions/citations 12–13px · wordmark 32–36px.

## Color
- **Background (pages):** `#FFFFFF` white — HARD RULE: page backgrounds stay white/near-white; strong colour lives only inside photo panels, badges, and packaging.
- **Stage/photo panel warmth:** butter `#F9E8B4` → honey `#F3D389` gradients, rounded 24–28px corners.
- **Primary text:** `#2B2115` warm dark brown-black (never pure black — keeps the warmth).
- **Muted text:** `#8C8272`.
- **Accent (the brand colour):** tomato `#E34A33` — wordmark, price badge, italic emphasis, CTA hovers. One red per viewport is plenty.
- **CTA:** ink `#2B2115` pill button, cream text. Red is identity, dark is action.
- **Metallic:** `#B9BEC4` aluminium — the tube only; the metal stays special.
- **Semantic (checkout/account):** success `#1F7A33`, warning `#B87400`, error `#B3170F`, info `#25566B`.
- **Dark mode:** none for marketing.

## Signature Assets (the ownables)
1. **The 26p Badge** — rotated (-7°) tomato circle, Fraunces 900, "26p / A DAY". The price never typeset politely. Successor to the reduced-sticker idea, now charming instead of cheap.
2. **The Sunny Stage** — product photographed/rendered inside a warm butter-gradient rounded panel on white. The tube leans at ~-6°, never perfectly upright.
3. **The Source Line** — every claim carries a superscript number resolving to an elegant italic citation at the section foot. Receipts as refinement, not grunge.
4. **The Tube Itself** — raw brushed aluminium, `Paste!` in tomato Fraunces running down it. The only product in the category that looks like art supplies.
5. **CTA as promise** — ink pill: "Hold your tube — £5 deposit". Always the same words. Never "Buy now".

## Photography (founder-set 2026-07-02/03)
- **People:** sunny, warm, beautiful. Golden morning light, film grain, attractive healthy people, relaxed and confident, deadpan-cool but NEVER tired, gloomy or miserable. Honest small amount of foam (the anti-fake-foam story told visually). Warm 70s Kodak palette.
- **Product:** the tube in real sunlight — on a windowsill, a sink edge, breakfast table. Golden hour, warm shadows. (The "seized exhibit" evidence-scan style is retired with Gutter Press; if evidence photography is ever needed, it must still be beautiful.)
- AI-generated people imagery = moodboard/ads-clearly-staged only; real humans for anything implying testimony.

## Spacing
- **Base unit:** 8px. **Density:** spacious and calm — whitespace is the premium signal. Scale: xs(4) sm(8) md(16) lg(24) xl(32) 2xl(48) 3xl(64).

## Layout
- **Approach:** hybrid — calm grid-disciplined structure with ONE playful element per viewport (the rotated badge, the leaning tube, an italic word). Poster-quality first viewport.
- **Grid:** 12-col desktop, max-width 1200–1240px; single column mobile.
- **Border radius:** photo panels 24–28px, buttons full pill, badges circular. Cards on white: none or 12px, no hard ink borders.

## Motion
- **Approach:** intentional. Badge drops in with one soft bounce (250ms), tube panel fades warm on load, italic emphasis words underline-draw on scroll (300ms). Nothing floaty, no parallax.
- **Easing:** enter ease-out; badge cubic-bezier(.2,.9,.3,1.2).
